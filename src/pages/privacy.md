_Last updated: July 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how BLD Southeast ("we," "us," "our") handles information when you visit bldsoutheast.com (the "Site").

## Information we collect

This Site is a static informational website. We do **not** run analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do **not** set cookies or collect personal information directly through the Site.

**Information you provide to third parties.** Event registration is handled entirely by TicketTailor, and speaker applications are handled by Google Forms. When you register for BLD Southeast or apply to speak, the information you submit (name, email, payment details, etc.) is collected and processed by those third-party services under their own privacy policies, not by us directly. We recommend reviewing TicketTailor's and Google's privacy policies before submitting information through those forms.

## Cookies

This Site does not set first-party cookies or use tracking technologies. Some third-party services you choose to visit (below) may set their own cookies once you follow a link to them.

## Third-party services

Following links from this Site takes you to services that operate under their own privacy policies:

- **TicketTailor** — event registration and ticketing.
- **Google Forms** — speaker applications.
- **TripZero** — optional attendee hotel booking and carbon-offset program.

Any information you provide to these services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Data retention

We do not maintain a database of attendee or applicant personal information — that data lives with our ticketing and form providers.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@bldsoutheast.com.
